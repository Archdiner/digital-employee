Use when opening a spreadsheet, board pack, management account, or financial statement to find numbers.

# Reading financial documents

## Where numbers live
- Management accounts and board packs: the summary or dashboard tab first, then the P&L, balance sheet, cash flow. The commentary pages explain the movements.
- Spreadsheets: check the tab names before reading cells. A tab named Summary, Dashboard, KPI, or Overview usually holds the reviewed figures. Tabs named Working, Calc, or Data hold inputs.
- Quarter columns are usually left to right, oldest to newest, or the reverse. Read the header row before reading a value.
- A number in brackets is negative. "k", "m", "bn" and "000s" in a header set the unit for the whole table.

## Definitions to watch
- EBITDA may be reported, adjusted, or run-rate. They differ. Record which one the source shows and keep them apart.
- Revenue may be gross or net, and may be a quarter, a year to date, or a last twelve months figure. Record the period exactly as the source labels it.
- Net debt definitions vary by lender. Take the source's figure and its label; do not recompute it.
- Plan, budget, and forecast are three different things. Record which one a variance is measured against.

## When recording a fact
- Entity: the company or fund exactly as the document names it.
- Metric: the source's own label, not a synonym.
- Period: the source's own label.
- Value: exactly as written, including brackets and decimals.
- Unit: from the table header or the cell.
- Location: sheet and row, or page and table, so a reader can find it again.
