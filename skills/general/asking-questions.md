Use when you hit something you do not know mid-task and must decide whether to ask or proceed.

# Asking questions

Ask, and wait, when:
- A term in the firm notes or documents is ambiguous and the answer changes a number or a table (which EBITDA, which fund, which quarter).
- You cannot find where a required set of numbers lives and the firm notes do not say.
- Two documents disagree on a figure that matters and neither is clearly later or final.

Proceed, and note it in open items, when:
- The gap affects wording, not numbers.
- A single minor figure is missing and the review still stands without it.
- You are choosing between two layouts and a past review settles it.

How to ask:
- One message. All your questions at once, numbered, each answerable in a sentence.
- Say what you will do if you get no answer.
- Never ask for something you could find by reading a document you already have.
