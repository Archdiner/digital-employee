Use when preparing data, schedules, reconciliations, or review materials for a regulatory filing, statutory return, or compliance report.

# Regulatory filing support

## Start from the form, not the data
1. Get the exact form or template version and the regulator's instructions for the period. Definitions in a return (what counts as an exposure, a related party, a liquid asset) come from the regulator's guidance, not from management reporting.
2. Build a mapping table: each line of the return, the definition that applies, the source system or schedule, and the person who owns it. This table is the deliverable behind the deliverable; reuse it next period.
3. Note the deadline, the submission route, and the authorised signatory from the firm notes.

## Populate
- Every figure traces to a fact or a computed fact with a formula. The return will be questioned line by line months later; the trail must survive that.
- Reconcile the return's totals to the financial statements or management accounts for the same date. Explain every reconciling item in a schedule.
- Compare to the prior submission. Movements above the firm's threshold get a one-line reason; a regulator's first query is always "why did this change".

## Review pack
Return, mapping table, reconciliation, prior-period comparison, open items. In that order. Mark it draft until the signatory approves.

## Never
- Never interpret a regulatory definition yourself when it is ambiguous. Write the two readings, the numbers under each, and ask.
- Never net items the form asks for gross.
- Never submit or transmit anything; prepare it for the person who does.
