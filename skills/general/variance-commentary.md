Use when explaining why a number moved: quarter on quarter, year on year, or against plan.

# Variance commentary

Order for each movement:
1. The number and the change. Both from facts, the change from the compute tool.
2. The one or two drivers the source documents give. Quote the document's reason, do not guess one.
3. Whether management expects it to persist, if the documents say.

Rules:
- One paragraph per metric that moved by more than the firm's threshold (see firm notes; default is 5% or any sign change).
- Do not explain a movement the documents do not explain. Write "no explanation given in the pack" and move on.
- Against plan: state the plan figure and the actual, both as facts, then the variance from compute.
- Never soften. "Revenue fell 8%" not "revenue was slightly softer".
